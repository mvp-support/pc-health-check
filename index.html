TheMVP PC Health Check


    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What it does
    
    
      
      
    • Lists your PC's parts first: processor, graphics card, memory, drives and motherboard.
      • 
      
    • Reads what your PC has already recorded: crashes, sudden shutdowns, hardware errors, drive health, temperatures and drivers.
      • 
      
    • Saves a report on your PC, with a plain-English summary, for you to send to TheMVP.
      • 
    
    
    
    What it does not do
    
    
      
      
    • It does not open your personal files, photos, browser history or passwords.
      • 
      
    • It does not send anything by itself. You decide whether to send the report.
      • 
      
    • Windows: it does not install, change or delete anything.
          Linux: it may install standard diagnostic tools (smartmontools, lm-sensors) from your distribution's own repository.
      • 
    
    
    
    The full source code is public in this repository. You can read every line before running it.
